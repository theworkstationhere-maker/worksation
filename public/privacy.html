Privacy

For each house we store the public Instagram ID you type and the town you pick (both shown on the public map), plus your email and a one-way hash of your password so you can log in. Your email is never shown or shared, and nobody (not even us) can read your password.

To stop fake sign-ups we also keep a scrambled fingerprint of your internet connection (never the address itself).

We never ask for a phone number, home address or payment. Your exact location is never collected: houses are placed on a made-up grid inside your town.

Please register only your own Instagram ID. To remove your house, message us on Instagram and we will delete it.

When you zoom in close, your browser loads street maps from OpenFreeMap (© OpenStreetMap contributors). Like any website, those servers see your internet address; we send them nothing else.

Map data: country outlines from Natural Earth via world-atlas; town search by Open-Meteo (CC BY 4.0); famous places from Wikidata (CC0). Built with Three.js.
